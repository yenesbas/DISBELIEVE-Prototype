DISBELIEVE - Prototype v1.0.0
=============================

TO PLAY
-------
Open play.html in any modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge).
That is the whole install. There is nothing to download, build or configure.

Click the page once when it loads so the browser lets the game play audio.

CONTROLS
--------
Move            Left / Right arrows, or A / D
Jump            Space, or W
Restart level   R
Fullscreen      F
Back to menu    ESC

THE ONE RULE
------------
Nothing is what it looks like. Platforms lie, floors vanish, and the spikes
are waiting for exactly the move you were about to make. Reach the green door.

MAKE YOUR OWN LEVELS
--------------------
MY LEVELS in the main menu opens a full level editor. It unlocks once you have
cleared every level of every chapter with a two-star average. The menu tells
you how far off you are. Full instructions are in src/editor/EDITOR_GUIDE.md.

More detail on everything: README.md and CHANGELOG.md in this folder.
